# Roadmap

## v1 · Android beta (now)
- Accurate voice notes in English, Bangla and mixed speech
- Notes filed automatically into six life areas: academic, business, skills, social, family, personal
- Universal voice command: one sentence becomes several tasks
- Built-in reminder engine: notification, alarm on silent, full-screen call-style alert; escalation; works offline and after restart; never duplicates
- Free beta for 20 to 50 testers

## v2 · Money
- Automatic capture of bKash, Nagad, Rocket and bank payments from notifications
- Several businesses, dues and who owes what; cash entries by voice

## v3 · Class mode
- Lecture recording with board photos, "didn't get it" markers, simple explanations and quizzes

## v4 · Inbox & auto-reply
- WhatsApp, Messenger, page comments and Gmail in one inbox
- Replies with permission or automatically, per contact group

## v5 · Circle & life story
- Family and friend circles: shared tasks, group chat, everyone's news, easy mode for parents
- Life story: one question a night, chapters, people who shaped you, PDF book

## Always
- Money, passwords and deleting always need a fingerprint
- Nothing is sent in the user's name before they see it, unless they turn on auto mode
- No secret tracking; each person chooses what to share

## Stack (planned)
React Native (Expo), on-device speech recognition, Gemini or Groq free tiers for AI, Supabase for sync and licences. Engines are swappable.
